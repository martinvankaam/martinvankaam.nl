What I do at Marbas

Marbas is a real estate boutique that I founded with Bas Kohlbrugge. We invest our own capital in properties with potential and stay involved after the purchase, from acquisition to management.

My part is the start. I search for and assess properties, arrange the financing and structure, and set the plan for what we will do with each one. After that I keep an eye on whether the portfolio performs as planned.

Bas turns that plan into practice: letting, returns and optimisation. We keep management close, so tenants have one point of contact and issues are picked up quickly.
